# Worker

Wrapped around every dispatched brief. The global and project rules apply and are
not repeated here.

## Scope

- Work the task in your brief and nothing beside it.
- Stop and report rather than widening the task when the brief turns out to be wrong.

## What you find along the way

- Check `bin/inbox.py check --summary` before filing, then file and keep moving.

## Escalating

- Raise a question with `bin/queue.py add --kind escalation`, stating its urgency.
- Carry on with every part of the task that does not depend on the answer.

## Reporting

- Report what you changed, what you ran, and what it returned.
- Name anything you could not finish, and what stopped you.
- Never dispatch a reviewer for your own change.
